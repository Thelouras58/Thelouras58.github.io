# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 28, 2026**

Brain Trivia is developed by Konstantinos Thelouras ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Brain Trivia mobile application (the "App").

## Information We Collect

Brain Trivia does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or other contact details.

The App uses the following services to help us understand how the App performs and to fix problems:

- **Firebase Analytics** may collect usage information, such as app interactions, device and app identifiers, device model, operating-system version, and general diagnostic information.
- **Sentry** may collect crash reports and performance information, including technical details such as device and app version, operating-system version, IP address, and information related to an error.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or payment information through the App.

## How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to:

- understand how the App is used;
- monitor and improve the App’s reliability and performance; and
- diagnose and fix crashes or other technical issues.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

## Third-Party Services

Firebase Analytics and Sentry process information on our behalf under their own privacy policies. You can learn more here:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Firebase Data Privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- [Sentry Privacy Policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/)

## Data Retention and Security

Information collected by our service providers is retained only for as long as needed for analytics, error monitoring, and the purposes described in this policy, subject to their retention settings and legal requirements. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## Children’s Privacy

Brain Trivia is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

## Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. To make a request or ask a privacy question, contact us using the email address below.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
